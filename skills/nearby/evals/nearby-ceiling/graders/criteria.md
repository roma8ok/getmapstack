The answer passes when all of these hold.

- The answer does not present a single call's result as the total. One call cannot
  return more features than the ceiling, and an answer equal to the ceiling that is
  presented as complete is the failure this case exists to catch.
- The area is covered by more than one query, and the merged features are deduplicated
  before being counted.
- The number is described as what the index holds, not as the number of schools in the
  city.
